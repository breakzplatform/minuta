# About the examples

These texts are here for trying Minuta: open one, switch to the night theme,
try the focus mode and the preview. They are in Portuguese and in the public
domain in Brazil.

- **Hino de Pernambuco**, the state anthem, with lyrics by Oscar Brandão da
  Rocha and music by Nicolino Milano. Official texts are not covered by
  copyright in Brazil (Law 9.610/1998, art. 8). Text from Wikisource.
- **Poema em linha reta** by Álvaro de Campos, a heteronym of Fernando
  Pessoa, who died in 1935. Text from Wikisource.
- **Luar do Sertão** (1914), with lyrics by Catulo da Paixão Cearense, who
  died in 1946. The melody is also credited to João Pernambuco, who died in
  1947; Catulo claimed the song as his alone, and who wrote the tune is
  still disputed. Text from Blocos Online.

To keep your own version of an example, use Save as and pick a folder of
yours. The installed examples are never changed.
